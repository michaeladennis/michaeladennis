## Hi, I'm Michaela Dennis!🌿

I am currently a graduate student at UC Santa Barbara pursuing a Master of Environmental Data Science Degree.

### Before Bren
I graduated from UCLA in 2026 with a B.S. in Environmental Science with a concentration in Environmental Systems and Society. I participated in the [Forests and Global Change Lab](https://sites.lifesci.ucla.edu/eeb-ordway/) where I worked on research projects using remotely sensed data to investigate land use change in the Congo Basin. I also worked alongside the [Ebony Project](https://www.cbi.ucla.edu/ebony-project), a tree-planting and ecological research initiative, for my senior capstone where my teammates and I assessed the project's impacts on local communities and ecosystems. Check out our final presentation [here](https://www.ioes.ucla.edu/project/the-ebony-project-in-cameroon/) for more information.
